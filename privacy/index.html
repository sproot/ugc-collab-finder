UGC Collab Finder · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

UGC Collab Finder is operated by individual entrepreneur Hunchenko Kateryna Olehivna (ФОП Гунченко Катерина Олегівна), Ukraine. Contact: katerinagunchenko2000@gmail.com.

What the app does

UGC Collab Finder is a private tool used by one UGC creator, the operator herself. It searches public Threads posts in which brands, shops and salons look for UGC creators, and sends the matching posts to the operator in a private Telegram chat together with a draft reply. The operator reads the draft, edits it and posts any reply herself.

The app never posts, replies, likes, follows or sends messages on Threads, and it does not act on behalf of anyone else.

What data we process

  • The operator's own Threads account, which authorizes the app: its Threads user ID, username and the access token Threads issues.
  • Public Threads posts returned by the Threads keyword search: post ID, text, link, author username, publication time and media type. Only posts that are already public on Threads are processed.

We do not collect private messages, contact lists, email addresses, phone numbers or any data from people who have not published a public post.

How we use it

  • To decide whether a public post is a request for a UGC creator, and how well it fits the operator's work.
  • To draft a reply the operator may send herself.
  • To avoid showing the operator the same post twice.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or build profiles of the people who wrote the posts.

Who processes it for us

  • Anthropic (Claude): the text of public posts is sent to Claude to classify the post and draft the reply.
  • Telegram: matched posts and drafts are delivered to the operator's private Telegram chat.

Nobody else receives the data.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on a private server under the operator's control. The access token is kept in a file that only the service account can read.

  • Post texts, extracted details and drafts are deleted after 90 days.
  • Post IDs are kept longer, without any text, only so that the same post is not shown again.
  • The access token is deleted when the operator revokes the app's access.

Your choices and rights

If you wrote a public Threads post and want us to delete anything we hold about it, or want to know what we hold, follow the steps on the data deletion page or write to katerinagunchenko2000@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used by them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
