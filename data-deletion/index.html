Data Deletion

UGC Collab Finder · Last updated: 16 November 2026

If you authorized UGC Collab Finder on Threads

  1. In Threads, open Settings → Account → Website permissions.
  2. Remove UGC Collab Finder.

The app then loses access to your account. We delete the access token and all data obtained with it within 30 days.

If you wrote a public Threads post

Write to katerinagunchenko2000@gmail.com with your Threads username or a link to the post. We delete everything we hold about it, including the post ID, within 30 days and confirm by email.

Even without a request, post texts and drafts are deleted automatically after 90 days. See the privacy policy.
