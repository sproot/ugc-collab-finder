UGC Collab Finder

A private tool for one UGC creator.

UGC Collab Finder searches public Threads posts in which brands, shops and salons look for UGC creators. It sends the matching posts to the creator in a private Telegram chat, together with a draft reply she can adapt and post herself.

The tool only reads public posts through the official Threads API. It never posts, replies, likes or follows on anyone's behalf.
